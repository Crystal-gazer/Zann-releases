# Zann — Local Risk & Scenario Forecasting (Beta)

**Zann** is a desktop risk modeling application developed by **Crystal Gazer Limited**. Designed for financial portfolios and physical capital projects (such as construction delays, infrastructure project overruns, or budget bottlenecks), Zann helps analysts and project managers foresee extreme, real-world tail risks ($p_{95}$ to $p_{99.9}$) before they happen.

Instead of forcing your data into idealized bell curves, Zann uses machine learning to preserve the authentic, often messy patterns of your historical data, giving you a realistic picture of worst-case scenarios and asymmetric risk.

---

## Why Zann?

* **100% Data Privacy (BYOC)**: Built on a "Bring Your Own Compute" model, Zann runs entirely on your local machine's hardware. Your sensitive spreadsheets and proprietary project data are never uploaded to the cloud or external servers.
* **Versatile Risk Modeling**: Works seamlessly for both financial asset exposures and physical operational risks.
* **Regulatory Audit-Ready**: Instantly export complete validation packages (execution logs, model parameters, and scenario tables) to satisfy internal governance or external regulatory reviews.

## Installation (Windows Beta)

1. Navigate to the **[Releases](../../releases)** page of this repository.
2. Download the latest Windows installer: `Zann_x64.exe`.
3. **Important Note for Beta Testers**: Because this early-access beta build is not yet digitally signed with an Extended Validation (EV) certificate, Windows Defender SmartScreen will flag the installer with an "Unknown Publisher" warning. 
4. To proceed with the installation safely, bypass the warning using either of these methods:
   * Click **More info** $\rightarrow$ **Run anyway** on the SmartScreen popup.
   * *Or*, right-click the downloaded `Zann_x64.exe` file $\rightarrow$ select **Properties** $\rightarrow$ check **Unblock** at the bottom of the General tab $\rightarrow$ click **Apply**.

## Quick Start Guide

### 1. Format Your Data
Prepare your historical data in a standard `.csv` file format. Zann's engine is agnostic to your column layout, meaning your target metric (e.g., `Loss_Amount`, `Delay_Days`, or `Cost_Overrun`) can be located anywhere in the spreadsheet.

Example `project_data.csv`:
```csv
Region,Contractor_Tier,Target_Variable,Site_Size,Vendor_Rating
North_NA,Tier_1,14.50,5,A
South_EU,Tier_2,8.10,2,B
North_NA,Tier_1,31.00,4,A
```

### 2. Train Your Local Engine
* Launch the Zann app and sign in with your active beta credentials.
* Import your CSV. Zann automatically groups your numerical data into clean categories (percentiles) and analyzes the strongest risk drivers without overloading your computer's memory.

### 3. Explore Scenarios
* Once the engine builds your risk matrix, use the dashboard filters to test different scenarios. 
* Compare your baseline target ($p_{50}$) against extreme, worst-case outcomes ($p_{80}$ to $p_{99.9}$) so you can confidently set capital buffers or schedule safety margins.
